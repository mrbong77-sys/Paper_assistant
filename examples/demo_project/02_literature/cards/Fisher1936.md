## What the work did
Fisher asked which linear combination of several measurements best separates two groups, and derived the linear discriminant function for this purpose. He illustrated it with four measurements of iris flowers from three species. The discriminant is the basis of linear discriminant analysis.

## Facts usable in the manuscript (with pages)
- The linear discriminant is the linear combination of measurements that best separates the group means relative to the within-group variation (쪽: 원문에서 확인).
